Types of PGMs

 

Bayesian networks (BNs) and influence diagrams (IDs) are two types of probabilistic graphical models widely used for building expert systems in several application domains. Both of them consist of acyclic directed graphs and probability distributions [1], [2], [3].

The main difference among them is that BNs only contain chance nodes, each representing a random variable, while IDs also contain decision nodes, which represent the options available to one or several decision makers, and utility nodes, which represent the decision makers’ preferences. As a consequence, BNs can only be used in diagnostic problems, while IDs can be used as decision support tools.
